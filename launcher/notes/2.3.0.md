• Bank accounts: add your checking and savings once, and their balances move with pay, bills, autopay, loan payments and spending. Automatic moves (a set amount each paycheck, or per purchase) are included.
• Home shows your accounts, where they're headed in the next 30 days, and the lowest point coming up.
