• Bank accounts: a split purchase now comes fully out of the account of whoever paid (and counts as one purchase for the $1 savings).
• Pay logged in both places (Pay and as income spending) counts once. One-time bonuses no longer trigger the per-paycheck savings moves.
• A job not logged for two pay periods is no longer projected as expected pay.
• Coming up and countdowns: the same date entered twice (like a holiday also added as an event) shows once.
